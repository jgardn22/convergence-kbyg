01

Land with a little breathing room

The official FAQ recommends Miami International Airport (MIA), about 12 miles away. Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL) is a farther alternative. Build a traffic buffer into airport transfers.

02

Reconfirm the stay, not the old deadline

The venue page shows inconsistent hotel-block deadlines that have both passed. Check live room availability or call (800) 548-8886 and reference “DUG Convergence.” Rates and inventory are not guaranteed.

03

Get the latest before you walk

Check organizer communications for badge pickup and the official catalog for room assignments. We are not guessing either. Bring a backup session for each slot.
