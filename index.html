What dates should I book?

Convergence runs December 8-11, 2026. The core conference is Wednesday and Thursday, December 9-10. Tuesday has the separately ticketed Partner Pre-Day plus the official Welcome Reception. Friday workshops are add-ons.

What is included in general admission?

The official FAQ includes the Tuesday Welcome Reception, core education sessions and executive roundtables, Expo Hall, Convergence-sponsored receptions, and breakfast, lunch, snacks and beverages on both core days. Partner-hosted events may have their own invitation or RSVP rules.

BDO, Vertex and eftsure list a docked event aboard The Miami Lady from 6:00-9:00 PM EST, overlapping Quisitive and Hummingbird from 6:00-8:00 PM. Check each host’s registration or RSVP requirements.

What should I wear?

Are Friday workshops included?

No. Microsoft-led workshops run Friday, December 11, 8:00 AM-1:00 PM ET, at $249 per attendee per workshop as currently listed. A separate ticket and a prepared environment in your organization’s tenant are required. Workshops overlap, so choose one.

The official FAQ says refunds are not available, but tickets can be transferred. Contact info@dynamicsusergroup.com about a possible future-event credit if you cannot attend or transfer.
